# Style Grammar: `shadowbox-moonlit` (3D Sculpted Moonlit Oval Shadow-Box)

Inspired by museum-grade multi-ply oval shadow-boxes with pleated origami mountains, cupped 3D lotus/chrysanthemum petals, warm glowing pavilion arches, and backlit creamy cloud rims.

- **Default `renderMode`:** `"sculpted3d"` (`fov: 24°`, `charFps: 12`).
- **Frame:** 3-ply stepped `oval_scallop_bottom` deep cavity (`#192133 → #26334d → #37496b`).
- **Lighting:** Cool-cream raking key light + luminous internal moonlight (`#ffebb8`) + warm pavilion hearth (`#ffab4d`) + seeded crevice GTAO (`0.88`).
- **Palette Rule:** Deep indigo-slate mountains (`#1a2e4a` – `#263e63`) contrasted against warm ivory/champagne foreground flora & clouds (`#faeed4`, `#dfb775`) with coral botanical accents (`#d96c5c`).
