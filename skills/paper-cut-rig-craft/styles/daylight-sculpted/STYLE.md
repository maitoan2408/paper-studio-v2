# Style Grammar: `daylight-sculpted` (3D Daylight Faceted Origami & Architectural Relief)

Inspired by crisp architectural paper sculptures, folded alpine cliff dioramas, and multi-tiered fairytale citadels bathed in directional 42° sunlight with deep cool-teal cast shadows.

- **Default `renderMode`:** `"sculpted3d"` (`fov: 24°`, smooth `60fps` or `24fps`).
- **Terrain:** Combine `folded_ridge` (`faceted`) with `tiered_cliff_slabs` (`capColor` meadow/snow ledges) and `tiered_pine_group`.
- **Lighting:** High-contrast raking sunlight (`[-6.4, 7.8, 9.8]`) + crisp `GTAO` crevice occlusion (`0.85`).
