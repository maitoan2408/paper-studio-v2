# Style Grammar: `winter-tunnel` (3D Concentric Cut-Paper Forest Tunnel)

Inspired by classic multi-ring tunnel books where 4–8 scalloped snow-blue paper arches step progressively inward toward a deep midnight starry clearing with pure white paper-cut pines and fairy/animal silhouettes.

- **Default `renderMode`:** `"sculpted3d"` (`fov: 24°`, `charFps: 12`).
- **Frame:** 4-ply wide-stepped concentric `scalloped_arch` (`#eff4fa → #b8d0ed → #6c98cf → #2e5c9a`) casting deep layered tunnel shadows.
- **Palette Rule:** Pure white (`#ffffff`) foreground trees & hero silhouettes contrasting sharply against royal cobalt (`#0c224d`) starry depth.
