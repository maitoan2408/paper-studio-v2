# Style Grammar: `lightbox-led` (3D Warm LED Backlit Heritage Lightbox)

Inspired by handcrafted Vietnamese / East Asian architectural paper lightboxes (e.g., *Chùa Cầu* shadow-boxes) where warm amber-gold LED strips hidden deep inside the box glow through translucent paper layers while dark foreground botanicals catch crisp white die-cut edge highlights.

- **Default `renderMode`:** `"sculpted3d"` (`fov: 24°`, `charFps: 12`, high `backlightGain: 1.65`).
- **Frame:** 3-ply `pagoda_lantern_window` dark lacquer border with crisp white-silver cut rim highlights.
- **Value Ladder:** Glowing amber/gold core (`z = -3.2..-1.8`) → warm rose/terracotta & timber architecture (`z = -1.2..-0.2`) → deep silhouette lotus/foliage foreground (`z = +0.5..+1.1`) with bright rim catchlights.
